Shadow Flip — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · App ID: com.pars.shadowflip

In short: Shadow Flip has no accounts and does not send your game progress anywhere — it is stored only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases. Those services process limited device data as described below.

1. Who we are

Shadow Flip (the "App") is a mobile game published by Oguzhan Bayrak ("we", "us"). Contact: oguzhan.byrk@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Your levels, stars, gems, unlocked items and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are saved locally on your device. We do not run servers for the App and do not receive this data. Uninstalling the App erases it. Settings → Reset progress restarts your levels and stars but keeps your gems, items and purchases.

If you turn on reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device (for example a daily reminder in the evening). They are created by the phone itself; no notification data or push token is sent to us or to any server. You can turn them off in the App's settings or in your phone's settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch them for an in-game reward) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To deliver, measure and protect ads, AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address, device model, operating system and language,
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and approximate location derived from the IP address.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice later from the App. On iOS, the App asks for permission before the IDFA can be used for tracking. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps.

Players who buy Remove Ads (also part of the Starter Pack) or have VIP see no ads at all: rewards that normally need a video are given right away.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To validate and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, the product and transaction identifiers and basic device information. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

The optional VIP subscription renews automatically until you cancel it. Billing, renewals and cancellation are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play in your account settings; RevenueCat only tells the App whether the subscription is active.

5. Analytics and crash reports

The App does not currently include separate analytics or crash-reporting services. If we add them, we will update this policy before the change ships.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad or purchase service, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store your data on our own servers, most requests concern the services above — contact us and we will help you exercise them, or use the controls in your device settings (reset advertising ID, limit ad tracking).

8. Data retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the App. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies linked above. All communication with these services is encrypted in transit.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the App.
