I bought something but don't have it

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and an active VIP subscription come back on any device signed in to the same App Store / Google Play account. Gems are added the moment a purchase completes; if one did not arrive, email us.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There are no accounts, so progress does not move between devices and is erased if you uninstall the game. Settings → Reset progress restarts your levels and stars but keeps your gems, items and purchases.

How do I cancel VIP?

VIP renews automatically until you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the period. iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You can also tap Manage subscription in the game's settings.

A video ad didn't play

Sometimes no video is available (for example offline). Wait a moment and try again; you are never charged for a video.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. In the EEA/UK you can change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy options.
